# Day 5 — acquisition channels

Goal: maximize probability of the first **paid $50** without paid ads, scraping, spam, or autonomous outreach.

## Priority order

1. **Upwork / global marketplaces — 40%**
   - Research fresh fixed-price technical jobs.
   - Prefer existing-code fixes, Flutter/API, WordPress, responsive bugs, small automation.
   - Prefer $50–$200, clear scope, <= 1 week, recent client activity, and low/moderate proposal count.
   - Reject jobs restricted to locations we cannot serve or jobs whose full realistic value is below $50.

2. **Mostaql + Khamsat / Arabic market — 35%**
   - Same technical scope, Arabic proposals.
   - Strong preference for clear existing-project fixes over full builds.
   - Scoring must not depend on English-only keywords.

3. **Direct outreach / Contra — 15%**
   - Only targeted, relevant outreach to businesses with a concrete observable technical problem.
   - No bulk messages or scraping.

4. **Experiments — 10%**
   - New channels only after the main channels produce enough evidence.
   - Moltbook remains optional/secondary; do not let it distract from paid-client channels.

## Pursuit rules

A lead enters the human-approval queue only when:
- deterministic classification is candidate or strong;
- realistic price ceiling is >= $50;
- the work is legal and platform-compliant;
- we can explain the solution in a short proposal;
- no account-sharing, policy-bypass, payment-routing, or credential-risk requirement exists.

Human approval is still mandatory before contacting a client or making any external/financial commitment.

## Daily operating target

- Research 15–25 fresh listings.
- Pre-screen obvious bad economics/location/skill mismatches before spending LLM calls.
- Run 8–12 plausible opportunities through Fix50.
- Keep the best 3–5 for proposal drafting.
- Optimize for replies and paid work, not number of records in the database.
